I’m pursuing a degree in computer science and a minor in AI engineering at BYU-Idaho. I’m especially interested in how thoughtful software and artificial intelligence can help people work through real-world problems. I enjoy the process of taking an idea, breaking it into manageable pieces, and building it into something people can use.

I’m continuing to grow as a developer by exploring web and mobile development, AI-powered tools, and better ways to design maintainable software. I’m excited to keep learning and to contribute to projects that make a meaningful difference.
